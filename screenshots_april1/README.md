## Screenshots — April 1 Capture Set

- **`close_look.png`**  
  Hubble mode, close fly‑in on Pillar 1 showing the near‑black dust core, warm umber body, and cream rim-glow with surrounding stars partially occluded.

- **`hubble_123.png`**  
  Hubble visible-light grading with all three pillars framed together: Anchor (Pillar 1, left), Spire (Pillar 2, center), and Fragment (Pillar 3, right) emerging from a shared dust base.

- **`hubble_vis.png`**  
  Wider Hubble-mode shot emphasizing the teal nebular background and the continuous “mountain range” of the Pillars of Creation in visible light.

- **`nebular_dust.png`**  
  Background-only composition: procedural star field plus teal/orange volumetric dust, no pillars in frame — used as the atmospheric backdrop for the scene.

- **`spawnpoint.png`**  
  Default spawn view just after entering the simulation: camera centered on Pillar 1 with HUD overlays (mission, instrument, coordinates, scale) visible. Good “hero” shot for the experience entry point.

- **`stellar_bubble.png`**  
  Appears as a bright stellar bubble full of points, but is actually the raw SDF volume shell.

- **`webb_infr.png`**  
  Webb near‑infrared palette applied to the all pillars, revealing more internal structure and allowing more background stars to shine through the gas.

- **`webb_infr_2.png`**  
  Mid‑zoom infrared shot highlighting Pillar 2’s sharp spire silhouette and ghostlier density, matching the JWST NIRCam look.

- **`webb_infr_2-3.png`**  
  Webb mode framing both Pillar 2 and Pillar 3, with the rightmost fragment pushed back in Z to read as a distant dust island behind the main complex.

- **`webb_infra_123.png`**  
  Full three-pillar composition in Webb infrared mode — warmer oranges, brighter hot tips, and thinner apparent dust walls so the surrounding star field remains prominent.
