## Screenshots — Pillars of Creation Spectral Explorer

- **volumetric-pillar-shell.png**  
  Hubble mode, debug view of the volumetric **ray-march container sphere** before the pillar mask and density shaping are applied. Looks like a glowing stellar cocoon, but in-engine it is the raw SDF volume shell.

- **nebula-starfield-background.png**  
  Hubble mode view of the **procedural star field** and **nebula background particles** with no pillars in frame. Represents background stars embedded in diffuse Eagle Nebula dust.

- **pillar1-closeup.png**  
  Close pass on Pillar 1 (Elephant Trunk) showing the near‑black core, warm umber body, cream ionized tip, and teal Fresnel rim in visible-light grading.

- **three-pillars-spawn.png**  
  Default spawn composition with all three volumetric pillars visible: Anchor (Pillar 1), Spire (Pillar 2), and Fragment (Pillar 3) emerging from a shared dust base.
